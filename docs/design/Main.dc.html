3 · Practices applied

  • Progressive disclosure. Grade, then six cards, then evidence.
  • Thumb zone. Scan, Listen and choices sit in the lower third.
  • Stream, never block. Grade and chips by 5 s; detail fills in after.
  • Legible type. Atkinson Hyperlegible body, 16 px minimum, scales to 200%.
  • Plain verdict words. None found, Check, Avoid. Never "safe".
  • Privacy in view. Lock state and "on this phone" on every family screen.

4 · Decisions for you

  1. Touch targets 48 px, above the SRS's 44 px. Gloves and one hand.
  2. Basket as a nav tab. It carries the north star; it costs one slot.
  3. Grade colours are our own, not Nutri-Score's. Thanuki to confirm.
  4. Auto-capture plus a shutter. Switch Access users need a button.
  5. Library saves by default, with a pause switch in Settings.
