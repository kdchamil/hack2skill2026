Build notes

  • Streaming. Render grade and chips first; cards fill in with skeletons. Announce with aria-live="polite", once.
  • Card row. One <a> per card: icon, title, ≤ 12-word headline, mini grade, tier badge.
  • Listen bar. Sticky above the nav. Play, language, speed 0.75 to 1.5×. Never autoplays.
  • Prompt sheet. Bottom sheet, focus moves in, Esc and swipe close it, "Not now" equal weight.
  • Motion. 150 to 250 ms ease-out; none under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Dark mode. Ground #0E1A18, surface #162522. Ships in v2 after a contrast check.
  • Copy. "May" and "consider". No "safe". No brand ranking. Price only with a budget.
  • Strings. All text from the i18n file; allow 40% growth for Tamil.
